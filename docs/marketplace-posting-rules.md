# Marketplace posting rules — where Watch Cartel content could cross-post

Reference doc for anyone (Michael, an affiliate, a VA) posting Watch Cartel — or
personal FS/WTB — content into outside watch communities. Pulled from public
policy pages and forum rule threads in Aug 2026; some primary pages sit behind
an egress block from this environment, so **verify current wording on the live
page before relying on it** — forum mods in particular change these often.

Two rules apply everywhere, regardless of what's below, and override anything
platform-specific:

- **Never list a watch as available/for sale if it isn't actually available.**
  This is FTC "bait advertising" (16 CFR §238): an insincere offer made to
  generate leads, with the intent to switch the buyer to something else once
  they respond (e.g. "sorry, that one just sold"). It's a federal deceptive-
  advertising violation, not merely a platform rule, and it's also the fastest
  way to get identified and blacklisted in a trading community — this niche is
  small and word travels.
- **Don't fabricate scarcity or a sale.** Real inventory, real WTB requests, or
  clearly-labeled "sourcing" posts only.

## Reddit — r/Watchexchange

- No official rules page was fetchable directly (Reddit blocks WebFetch from
  this environment) — confirm current text at `reddit.com/r/Watchexchange`
  sidebar/wiki before posting.
- General knowledge / secondhand reporting: self-promotion and referral/
  affiliate links are treated as spam and removed; accounts need karma/age
  minimums before the sub allows posting; items should be photographed in
  hand at time of listing (proves possession); "sold" posts must be flaired
  as sold promptly, not left up as bait.
- Practical read: this sub is for peer-to-peer trades, not for a business
  running its own promo through listings. A link back to `thewatchcartel.com`
  in a listing body reads as advertising and risks removal/ban, independent
  of whether the watch itself is real.

## Reddit — r/Watches (general sub)

- Standard Reddit self-promotion norms apply: no link-only posts, no
  referral/affiliate links, disclose any commercial relationship per Reddit's
  site-wide self-promotion and sockpuppeting policies. Community, not a
  marketplace — off-topic for FS/WTB posts at all.

## WatchUSeek — Sales Corner

Source: [Sales Corner Rules & Guidelines](https://www.watchuseek.com/threads/sales-corner-rules-and-guidelines.5212475/)

- Private sellers need the account to be **90+ days old with 100+ posts** in
  the discussion forums before Sales Corner access unlocks — "speed posting"
  to hit the threshold gets access pulled.
- Commercial links **inside a post body** are allowed; links in a **signature
  or profile** are not, and banner/avatar advertising is Sponsor-only.
- No eBay/auction links in the Sales Corner — auctions go in a separate forum.
- Offers must go through PM/email; **replying directly on the thread to make
  an offer gets removed and infracted.**
- Practical read: a real listing with "also flows through thewatchcartel.com"
  in the post body is plausible here once the account is established; it
  cannot live in a signature line.

## Omega Forums / Rolex Forums — private sales

- Terms prohibit "spam and soliciting of business by persons not approved by"
  the forum, while explicitly carving out ordinary member-to-member trading
  through the forum's own sales tools. Each forum runs its own posting
  ruleset for photos, pricing, and bump frequency — check the pinned
  "PLEASE READ" threads in each sales subforum before posting
  ([Omega dealer rules](https://omegaforums.net/threads/please-read-dealer-sales-and-posting-rules-for-all-listings-includes-replies.54179/),
  [Omega private seller rules](https://omegaforums.net/threads/please-read-private-sellers-buyers-sales-rules-for-all-listings-inc-parts-wtb.54177/)).
- Practical read: running Watch Cartel promo through these forums under an
  unapproved account is exactly what "soliciting business" is meant to catch.

## Chrono24

- Private-seller listings go through an inspection that requires **proof-of-
  ownership photos** (watch photographed with a specified code/time) before
  publication.
- Dealer accounts must carry **10+ watches available for immediate sale** and
  fulfill "in stock" orders within 3 business days — i.e., "in stock" is a
  status Chrono24 expects to be literally true, not aspirational.
- Practical read: Chrono24 is built around verified possession. A listing for
  a watch not in hand fails inspection or misrepresents dealer stock status —
  higher friction to fake here than on a forum, by design.

## eBay

Sources: [Links policy](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/links-policy?id=4248),
[Fee avoidance / offering to buy-sell outside eBay](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/payment-policies/offers-buy-sell-outside-ebay-policy?id=4272)

- **No links to any site other than eBay anywhere in a listing — even non-
  clickable text URLs are prohibited.** A "crossposted to thewatchcartel.com"
  line in an eBay listing violates this outright.
- Referencing contact info, or asking for the buyer's, in the context of
  transacting outside eBay makes the seller liable for final-value fees even
  if the item didn't sell on eBay — this is actively enforced, not
  theoretical.
- Practical read: eBay is the least compatible platform for cross-post
  attribution. Any Watch Cartel mention has to live outside the listing
  entirely (seller "About" page only, which eBay does allow to a limited
  degree — verify current About-page rules before using it).

## Facebook Marketplace & Groups

- Marketplace itself is scoped to **consumer-to-consumer** sales; using it in
  a business/commercial capacity is restricted in some regions (EEA,
  Philippines, India) and can trigger listing removal or suspension.
- Non-physical items — services, subscriptions, digital products, "in search
  of" posts — are banned from Marketplace outright. A Watch Cartel membership
  pitch itself cannot be listed as a Marketplace item; only a physical watch
  can be.
- Groups (e.g. regional watch trading groups) run their own admin rules
  independent of Marketplace's commerce policy — check each group's pinned
  rules for self-promo/referral-link tolerance before posting.

## Instagram / TikTok

- Not marketplaces in the same sense, but both have shopping/commerce
  surfaces with explicit misleading-content rules: no false claims about a
  product's status, affiliation, or availability, and no promoted price/deal
  that isn't currently valid.
- Practical read: a story/post that teases a watch as "available" when it
  isn't, or that keeps running after the "deal" is gone, is a policy
  violation on these platforms too, separate from the FTC issue.

## Net takeaway for a posting playbook

- Fake-in-hand / "it sold" bait listings are out everywhere — by rule on
  vetted marketplaces (Chrono24), by norm on trust-based forums (WatchUSeek,
  Omega/Rolex Forums, r/Watchexchange), and by federal law regardless of
  venue.
- Off-platform links in the listing body are banned outright on eBay, banned
  in signatures/profiles on WatchUSeek, and against the spirit of Reddit's
  self-promo rules. They're workable in a WatchUSeek post body once the
  account is established, and generally fine as a bio/profile link on
  Instagram/TikTok.
- See `docs/crosspost-playbook.md` for how to get Watch Cartel distribution
  out of this without violating any of the above.
