The non-negotiables

What this desk will never do.

Most of what goes wrong in this trade goes wrong for the same handful of reasons. These are ours, in writing, before you have paid anything.

✗

We will never tell you we have it. Before a watch that is not ours is offered to you, it is confirmed available. Listings in this market go stale in hours; relaying one as offerable is how brokers burn a reputation they only get to burn once.

✗

We will never compare two prices that are not comparable. BNIB is not "like new," and "retail ready" means polished to look better than it is. Full set and watch-only are two different assets. If a comparison crosses those lines, we say so instead of quietly showing you a spread that is not there.

✗

We will never count quote-chasing as supply. NTQ and LTQ are price discovery, not offers, and they are excluded from every number we give you. Counting them inflates apparent supply and invents spreads that do not exist.

✗

We will never expose the members. No public member list, no screenshots of your deals, no forwarding your ask around the market with your name attached. Discretion is not a feature here, it is the container.

✗

We will never push you past a payment you can recover. Escrow or a protected rail on anything that warrants it, deposits before travel, and no fronting money into a handshake. That advice costs us deals and we give it anyway.
